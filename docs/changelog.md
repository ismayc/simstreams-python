# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release. `sim_template()`, `sim_template_serial()`, `make_generators()`,
`split_reps()`, `make_set_up()`, the book's `pi_worker()` and
`cauchy_worker()`, `sim_template_coupled()` with the Chapter 6 epidemic case
study, `benchmark()` and `benchmark_cores()`, and the toy `PCG8` generator.

The test suite pins the Chapter 4 and Chapter 6 results printed in the book
and covers every line; `pytest` fails below 100%.
